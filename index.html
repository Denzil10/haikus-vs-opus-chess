5 Claude Haikus (voting) vs 1 Claude Opus: chess, no tools

Three games played on 2026-11-19. Every move is a fresh claude -p --tools "" call (no tools, no code, no memory between moves) that gets the move list, the FEN and a text diagram. The team: five independent Claude Haiku 5.5 calls per move, none sees another's answer; illegal answers are dropped, the move with the most votes is played, and a tie goes to the tied move named by the lowest-numbered Haiku. Referee: match.py (python-chess). Afterwards Stockfish (depth 18, analyze.py) graded every move; "loss" is centipawns lost by that move, clipped at ±1000.
